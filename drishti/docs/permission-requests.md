# Permission requests (drafts)

Two government sites say their material may be reused only with permission. These are ready-to-send drafts. Send them from your own email, keep the replies, and note the answer in `DATA_SOURCES.md`.

Checked on 26 Oct 2026. Not legal advice.

## 1. Jan Aushadhi store list (PMBI)

- **Why:** janaushadhi.gov.in/copyright says material "may be reproduced free of charge after taking proper permission by sending a mail to us", with the source prominently acknowledged.
- **To:** complaints@janaushadhi.gov.in (the only email listed on the site; office phone 011-49431800)
- **Subject:** Permission to show Jan Aushadhi Kendra locations on a free public map

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am Falak Patel, a pharmacist from Bharuch, Gujarat. I am building aushadhi-drishti, a free, open source map that shows where generic medicine stores are and where more are needed:

- Map: https://falakpat3l.github.io/aushadhi-drishti/
- Code: https://github.com/falakpat3l/aushadhi-drishti

Today the map shows only the official district counts of Kendras from the Rajya Sabha answer of February 2026.

I request permission to show, from the "Locate nearest Kendra" list on janaushadhi.gov.in, only these fields for each Kendra: store code, address, pincode and district. I will not copy or show owner names, phone numbers or any other personal details. The data would be refreshed at most once a week, the source would be acknowledged on the map and in the project's data notes, and I would remove anything on request.

If there is a preferred way to receive this list (for example a file or an open data link), I would be glad to use that instead.

Thank you,
Falak Patel
Bharuch, Gujarat
https://github.com/falakpat3l

## 2. CDSCO drug quality alerts

- **Why:** cdsco.gov.in Copyright Policy says its contents "may not be reproduced partially or fully, without due permission", and the source must be acknowledged when referred to.
- **What the map does without permission:** counts only (how many samples per month, per district or reporting state, and per generic name), each linked to the official CDSCO list. This request is for showing more than counts.
- **To:** dci@nic.in, copy to ranga.cs@cdsco.nic.in (Web Information Manager, as listed on the CDSCO Contact Us page)
- **Subject:** Permission to show NSQ and spurious drug alerts on a free public map

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am Falak Patel, a pharmacist from Bharuch, Gujarat. I am building aushadhi-drishti, a free, open source map of India's medicine supply side made only from public data:

- Map: https://falakpat3l.github.io/aushadhi-drishti/
- Code: https://github.com/falakpat3l/aushadhi-drishti

Following CDSCO's copyright policy, the map now shows only counts of not-of-standard-quality and spurious samples by month and district, with a link to each official CDSCO list.

I request permission to show, for each sample in CDSCO's monthly alert lists and on the public NSQ portal (cdscoonline.gov.in): the product name, batch number, result and reporting month, exactly as published, with CDSCO acknowledged as the source and a link to the original list. The aim is to help pharmacists and patients find these alerts more easily. Each entry would carry the note that a not-of-standard-quality result applies to one batch only, and spurious samples would never be shown against the company named on the label.

If CDSCO offers this data as a file or an open data feed, I would be glad to use that instead.

Thank you,
Falak Patel
Bharuch, Gujarat
https://github.com/falakpat3l
